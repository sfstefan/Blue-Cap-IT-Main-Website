Verkada's October 2026 product update is one of its broadest releases yet. Software updates roll out on **October 8** unless otherwise noted, and this time Verkada isn't just improving existing products — it's launching **two entirely new product lines**: Verkada Transportation for vehicles and Verkada Sound Systems for audio. Add a dual-spectrum thermal camera, a new alarm panel for smaller sites, a touchscreen intercom, 3D maps, and an AI connector that links Command to tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, and there's a lot to cover. Here's everything that's new.

## Two New Product Lines

### Verkada Transportation

**Verkada Transportation** brings video security and fleet management to vehicles. The integrated solution includes **three cameras, a gateway, and a dedicated Command experience** — so organizations running buses, delivery vans, or service fleets can manage vehicle video alongside the rest of their Verkada deployment instead of in a separate system.

### Verkada Sound Systems

**Verkada Sound Systems** delivers cloud-managed audio for **ambient music and announcements** from a single platform. It launches with two hardware lines: **Network Speakers** and **Network Audio Players**. For businesses that already manage cameras, doors, and alarms in Command, overhead audio can now live in the same place.

## Cameras: Thermal Imaging, Government-Grade Models & Smarter AI

### CTB63 Dual-Spectrum Thermal Bullet Camera

The **CTB63** is Verkada's first dual-spectrum thermal camera, available in **wide-angle and telephoto** variants. It captures standard and thermal feeds at the same time, provides **temperature insights**, and runs **people and vehicle analytics** — a strong fit for perimeters, industrial sites, and areas where lighting is unreliable.

### CR63-TE Telephoto Remote Camera

The **CR63-TE** delivers long-distance **4K coverage** for hard-to-wire locations, with a **built-in LTE modem** and multiple power supply options. Think parking lots, construction sites, and remote gates where running cable isn't practical.

### Six Government-Grade Cameras

Verkada added six government-grade models that meet the **FIPS 140** encryption standard and are **TAA and FY2019 NDAA compliant**:

- **CH53-E-G and CH63-E-G** four-camera multisensors
- **CY53-E-G and CY63-E-G** two-camera multisensors
- **CR63-E-G and CR63-TE-G** remote cameras

If your organization works with federal, state, or local government — or has procurement rules that require these certifications — this significantly expands your options.

### Introducing Catalyst, the Verkada MCP

**Catalyst** connects your Command organization to AI platforms including **Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini**, letting teams build custom workflows, reports, and integrations on top of their Verkada data. Catalyst is launching as a **private beta**.

### AI Search & New Alerts

- A **new AI-powered search model** is more accurate for safety-related searches and supports **multi-condition queries** using "and" or "not"
- **New alerting capabilities** detect individuals **concealing or grabbing items and running**, **obstructed doors**, and **wrong-way driving**
- **LPR and Analytics Mode** lets third-generation bullet cameras run people and vehicle analytics **while capturing license plates** at the same time
- **Transport Number Mode** lets you search by **shipping container IDs** on trucks and trailers — useful for logistics yards and loading docks

### Other Camera Updates

- **Unified Timeline improvements:** live mode, Access Control integration, and a more immersive maps view
- **Telescoping Ceiling Mount** positions cameras up to **66 in / 167 cm** below a ceiling or overhang
- **[Command Connector](../command-connector.html) improvements:** passive ONVIF mode and H.265 support for third-party cameras
- **Viewing Station improvements:** watch Verkada Alarms events as they unfold, cast streams from Command to a viewing station, and save preset layouts
- **Incident Management improvements:** add Access Control events to incident reports, use the incident tab in the Command mobile app, and export reports directly to Auror
- **Standardized API endpoints** with consistent field names across all product lines
- A **Lens Distortion Correction (LDC) toggle** in camera settings

## Alarms: A Panel for Smaller Sites & Fire Alarm Monitoring

### BP42 Alarm Panel & Better Sensor Protocol

The new **BP42 Alarm Panel** is built for smaller sites, with **10 wired inputs** plus support for **Better Sensor Protocol (BSP)** addressable sensors. BSP itself is a major upgrade: it supports up to **200 doors or 50 powered motion sensors on a single wire run**, which can dramatically reduce wiring on larger installs.

New sensors include the **QM11-R** BSP wired wall motion sensor, **QM21-R** BSP wired ceiling motion sensor, **QM21-W** wireless ceiling motion sensor, and the **QB11 panic button**.

### Fire Alarm Monitoring

**Fire Alarm Monitoring** sends real-time **SMS and email alerts tied to live camera feeds**, along with centralized system health management — so you can see what's happening the moment a fire alarm trips.

### Smarter Alarm Response

- **Lockdown as Alarm Response** automatically locks down a site when a panic button is pressed
- **Late to Arm/Disarm alerts** notify you if a site isn't armed or disarmed by its scheduled time
- **Maps + Verkada Monitoring** places devices on Command maps so monitoring agents can communicate where an intruder was last seen
- **Easier multi-site management** with centralized user keycodes, a maintenance-focused system health dashboard, and auto re-arm for inactive spaces
- **Alarm Panel Expanders** allow more than one alarm panel per Command site for larger buildings
- **Cameras-only, self-monitored alarms** are now available globally

### Heads-Up: Classic Alarms End-of-Life

Verkada **Classic Alarms** reached global **end-of-sale on August 1, 2026**, and will reach **end-of-life on August 1, 2027**. If you're still running Classic Alarms, now is the time to plan your migration — and the new BP42 panel and BSP sensors make that transition easier for smaller sites.

## Access Control: Wireless Controller & a Mobile SDK

- The **AW33 Wireless Access Controller** combines a door controller and wireless hub into one device, streamlining **AL54 wireless lock** deployments
- The **Pass SDK** lets organizations embed Verkada door access into their **own iOS and Android apps**
- **Access Control events** now appear in the **Unified Timeline** and in the new **Command Maps**
- **Custom roles** let admins build and assign permissions tailored to their team

## Intercom: TK74 Touchscreen & AI Receivers

- The **TK74 Touchscreen Intercom** features a **7" touchscreen**, **multitenant support**, and credential options including **Face Unlock** — ideal for multi-tenant buildings and shared lobbies
- **AI receivers** let admins deploy **voice AI** into intercom call flows for context-aware conversations with visitors
- A **global receivers page** centralizes receiver management, with a **"here / away" status** for each receiver
- Intercoms are now viewable in **Command Maps**, and **custom roles** are supported

## Command Platform: 3D Maps & Actionable Alerts

### Maps in Command (Beta)

**Maps in Command** is a **3D digital twin** that unifies cameras, doors, and alarms on one interactive floor plan. Combined with access control, intercom, and alarm devices now appearing on maps, this gives security teams a single visual picture of an entire site. Maps is currently **in beta**.

### Actionable Alerts & Permissions

- **Actionable alerts** add collective notification management, real-time response tracking, and clear audit trails
- A new **alert recipient permission setting** toggles between broadcast delivery to all recipients or auth-scoped delivery that follows each user's permissions
- **Organization-level custom roles** — for example, roles that can manage users or set up devices

## Workplace: Employee Check-In & a Refreshed Kiosk

- **Employee check-in** lets visiting staff sign in across a multi-location organization, either by Employee ID at the kiosk or automatically when they open a designated door
- A **redesigned kiosk tablet experience** with a simplified welcome screen
- **Off-tablet Face Unlock enrollment** lets visitors enroll from their own devices while signing in or registering for an event
- **Create Guest events in Command** to organize, update, and cancel upcoming visitor events
- **Offline device alerts** when a Guest tablet or printer goes offline
- **Custom roles** for Workplace, including access to Global Approved Lists, the Global Deny List, and organization settings
- **Italian and Somali** language support for Guest Types and Approved List applications
