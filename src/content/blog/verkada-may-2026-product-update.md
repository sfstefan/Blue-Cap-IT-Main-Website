Verkada's May 2026 product update lands with general availability on May 14, bringing a brand-new generation of bullet cameras, expanded AI capabilities, next-generation access controllers, and a slate of refinements across Command, Intercoms, Workplace, and Connectivity. Here's everything that's new.

## Cameras: A New Generation of Bullet Cameras

### Third-Generation Bullet Cameras

The headline hardware release is the **third-generation Verkada bullet cameras**, redesigned from the ground up with advanced on-camera analytics, improved image quality, streamlined installation, and new optional attachments for site-specific deployments. If you've been considering an outdoor camera refresh, this is the line to evaluate.

### Inactivity Detection

A new **inactivity detection** capability fires alerts when people or vehicle activity is *not* observed over a specified time window — useful for staffing gaps, abandoned equipment monitoring, and after-hours coverage verification.

### Mobile Improvements

- **Redesigned video player on mobile** with touch-based controls *(Available May 26)*
- **Favorite Sites** feature in the Command mobile app for one-tap navigation to your most-watched cameras
- Ability to **update IP addresses for existing Command Connector channels** without full reconfiguration

## Alarms: AI Deterrence Expands to Third-Party Cameras

### AI Deterrence on Any Camera

The big news for alarms is that **AI-powered deterrence** — the human-like voice warnings that respond intelligently to detected threats — now works on **third-party cameras through Command Connector**. If you've kept legacy cameras connected to Verkada, you can now layer AI deterrence on top of them without replacing hardware.

### Vehicle Line Crossing & Advanced Triggers

**Vehicle line-crossing triggers** can now activate alarms when vehicles cross digital lines in specified directions — ideal for parking lots, restricted driveways, and loading dock monitoring. Combined with **loitering** triggers, these advanced detections are now also supported on third-party cameras via Command Connector.

### Alarm Management Refinements

- **Alarm event reports** with filtering by time, location, device type, and dispatch status
- **Temporary muting** of specific sensors or cameras without disarming the full system
- **Natural language schedule setup** for arm/disarm scheduling — describe the schedule in plain English and Command builds it

## Access Control: New Controllers, Smarter Mobile, Emergency Workflows

### AC43 & AC43-G Four-Door Controllers

The new **AC43 and AC43-G** four-door controllers feature redesigned circuitry, **LTE backup** for cellular failover, and next-generation battery technology — a meaningful step up for multi-door deployments and sites where wired backhaul reliability is a concern.

### EV3 Multi-Technology Card

The **ACC-AC-EV3-MT-1 EV3 Multi-Technology Card** combines encrypted and proximity credential technologies in a single card — easier transition for sites migrating from legacy prox systems.

### Emergency Response Built In

Verkada now includes **emergency scenario functionality based on the Standard Response Protocol** developed by the "I Love U Guys" Foundation — the framework widely used in K-12 education. This brings standardized lockdown, evacuation, and shelter-in-place workflows directly into Command.

### Wireless Lock Enhancements

- **Disconnection alerts** for wireless locks
- **Static cache** for local credential storage and offline access when wireless connectivity drops
- **Custom polling frequencies** (adjustable from the default 10-second interval) for battery optimization

### Other Access Control Updates

- **Credential modification webhook** for syncing changes to third-party systems
- New **Visitor Access page** for managing temporary door access
- **Directory-scoped badge templating** with delegated permissions
- **AF64 Access Station Pro** is now generally available for purchase

## Intercom: Privacy Controls & Zoom Integration

- **Privacy controls** allow disabling continuous streaming outside active calls, plus configurable audio retention
- **Zoom Phone Interop certification** achieved — Verkada intercoms can now route calls into Zoom-based phone systems
- **Refreshed Command web calling interface** with improved layouts and clearer transcripts
- **HEVC support** delivers higher video resolution and sharper digital zoom without increased bandwidth requirements

## Command Platform: AI-Powered Search & Better Permissions

- **Audit log natural language search** — use plain language to filter and find audit events *(Available end of May)*
- **Unified user and group pages** for centralized permission tracking and assignment
- **Custom site roles** for granular Video Security and Workplace permissions *(Available end of May)*

## Workplace: Healthcare Workflows & Document Signing

- **Signed document fields** in Verkada Guest let visitors fill in PDF information as part of check-in
- **Global approved lists** for centralized multi-site visitor management
- **Visitor activity reports** with CSV export
- **Healthcare-specific updates** including separate workflows for adults and minors, custom questionnaires, integrated document signing, and **Face Match against internal deny lists**
- **Receptionist-assisted check-in** — front desk staff can run visitor workflows directly from a computer

## Connectivity

- **Point-to-point support** on **GW31-E Outdoor Wi-Fi Gateways**, allowing them to serve as wireless access points in addition to backhaul
